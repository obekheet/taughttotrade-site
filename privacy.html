Privacy

Taught to Trade collects as little as it can. This page lists everything.

If you subscribe by email

Your email address is stored by Kit (kit.com), the service that sends our emails. We use it only to send Taught to Trade emails: new teardowns, new free tools, and the occasional note about our own products. We never sell or share it. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future sends.

Visit counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint you and does not follow you across other sites. We see totals such as page, country, browser and referring site.

The repaint checker

The repaint checker runs entirely in your browser. Code you paste into it is not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Some pages carry a labelled TradingView affiliate link. If you click it, TradingView records the referral on its own site under its own privacy policy. We see totals only, never who you are.

Paid products and the Teardown Letter are sold through Gumroad, which handles payment details under its own privacy policy. We see the order, your email address and the name you give Gumroad.

Questions

Reply to any email we send and it reaches us.
